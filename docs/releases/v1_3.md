# MyWallet 1.3

Release notes for the 1.3 line. Each patch release (1.3.1, 1.3.2, ...) is added at the top of this
file.

## 1.3.0 (2026-10-17)

Income fees, expenses paid partly or entirely later, multiple transaction filters and an Arabic
interface. Deductions support subcategories, and startup and loading screens carry the app's branding.

### Income fees and deductions
- Add a fixed or percentage fee to income. The preview shows what the account receives after the
  fee and deductions. An income of 1,000 with a fee of 20 adds 980 to the account.
- Fees and deductions together cannot exceed the income amount.
- Choose a deduction's category and then its subcategory. These links remain when the entry is
  reopened or restored from a backup.
- Income rows keep the title readable, with deduction details on a separate line.

### Expenses paid later
- Switch on Paid later to enter what you paid now, who you owe and an optional due date. The
  remainder appears in Debts, linked to the expense.
- An expense of 1,000 with 300 paid now and a fee of 3 takes 303 from the account and records 700
  owed. Paying nothing now leaves the account unchanged and records the full 1,000 owed.
- Change the paid-now amount when editing the expense; its linked amount owed updates with it.
- Fees apply to the amount paid now. A fee cannot be added when nothing is paid now.

### Transaction filters
- Select several types, accounts and categories using the type chips and filter checklists.
- Selections within a group match any selected choice; the groups apply together. For example,
  select two accounts and Expense to see expenses involving either account.
- Category filters show matching parts of an entry, including deductions from income.
- Clear a group with All, or reset every filter with Clear all. If a new entry is saved outside the
  current filter, use Show to reveal it.

### Arabic and text direction
- The interface is available in Arabic and follows right-to-left layout.
- Amount input accepts Arabic and Persian digits. Displayed money uses Latin digits.
- Your saved names and notes keep the text you entered, and exports keep their existing format.

### Startup, loading and About
- Startup shows one splash with the MyWallet logo in the center and copyright at the bottom,
  following the app theme where supported by Android.
- Full-screen loading states use the animated Aristonis brand loader, with English and Arabic
  text. Fast loads skip the loader, and system animation settings are respected.
- About includes attribution and a link to star MyWallet on GitHub.

### Upgrading
- The database schema is unchanged from 1.2.0.
- Export a backup from Settings before you update, as with any update.
- Backups from 1.2 restore in 1.3. New backups use format 3 to preserve the expanded entries;
  MyWallet 1.2 cannot restore these backups. Older supported backups remain readable.
